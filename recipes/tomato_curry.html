Tomato Curry

A tangy and spicy South Indian Tomato Curry. Lightly mashed tomatoes cooked with a fragrant tempering of mustard, fenugreek, cumin, and curry leaves. Quick, easy, and absolutely delicious!

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 15 mins
Total Time 25 mins
Servings 3

Ingredients

  • For Tempering:
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ¼ tsp fenugreek seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 1 dry red chilli
  • Aromatics & Vegetables:
  • 3 green chillies, slit
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 cup tomatoes, chopped
  • Small piece of tamarind (optional, use if using sweet hybrid tomatoes)
  • Spices & Garnish:
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin (jeera) powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

Tamarind Tip

If you are using sour country tomatoes (desi tomatoes), you can skip the tamarind. For sweet hybrid tomatoes, a small piece of tamarind balances the sweet profile perfectly.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and temper: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the fenugreek seeds, cumin seeds, mustard seeds, curry leaves, and dry red chilli. Let them splutter and release their aroma.
  2. Sauté aromatics: Add the green chillies, chopped onion, and minced garlic. Sauté for a few minutes until the onion softens and turns translucent.
  3. Cook tomatoes: Stir in the turmeric powder and chopped tomatoes. If you are using sweet hybrid tomatoes, add the small piece of tamarind at this point. Cover and cook on medium heat for 5 minutes.
  4. Add spices: Add salt, red chilli powder, roasted cumin powder, and coriander powder. Mix well, cover, and cook for another 5 minutes until the tomatoes are completely soft.
  5. Mash & thicken: Remove the cover and lightly mash the tomatoes using the back of your spatula. Cook for 2-3 more minutes until the curry thickens and comes together.
  6. Serve: Stir in the chopped fresh coriander leaves. Turn off the heat and serve hot with rice, dosa, or idli.
