Simple Andhra Palakura Pappu

A classic, everyday Andhra-style spinach dal. In this version, the dal and spinach are pressure-cooked together, mashed, and enhanced with a fragrant tempering of garlic and popu dinusulu.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Total Time 30 mins
Servings 3–4

Ingredients

  • For Pressure Cooking:
  • 1 cup toor dal
  • 2 cups water
  • 2–3 cups chopped spinach
  • 3–4 green chilies
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Additives:
  • 2–3 tbsp tamarind juice
  • Tempering (Popu):
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp popu dinusulu (or ½ tsp mustard & ½ tsp cumin seeds)
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 green chilies, split
  • Curry leaves
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • Garnish:
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

What is Popu Dinusulu?

Popu dinusulu is a traditional South Indian tempering spice mix consisting of mustard seeds, cumin seeds, urad dal, and chana dal.

Instructions

  1. Pressure cook: Add toor dal, water, chopped spinach, green chilies, salt, and 1 tsp oil to a pressure cooker. Cook for 3–4 whistles until the dal is soft.
  2. Mash: Open the cooker once the pressure releases, and mash the dal-spinach mixture until smooth using a pappugutti (traditional dal masher) or a heavy spoon.
  3. Add tamarind: Add the tamarind juice to the mashed dal and mix well.
  4. Prepare tempering: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a separate small pan. Add the popu dinusulu, crushed garlic cloves, green chilies, and curry leaves. Fry until the garlic starts to brown and release its aroma.
  5. Sauté onions: Add the chopped onion to the tempering and sauté until they turn golden brown.
  6. Combine: Pour the hot tempering mixture directly into the mashed dal.
  7. Simmer: Stir in the fresh coriander and simmer the dal on low heat for 5 minutes. Serve hot.
