Chicken Masala

A delicious, high-protein chicken masala recipe.

Prep Time 5 mins
Cook Time 12 mins
Total Time 17 mins
Servings 2
Calories 395 kcal

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp tandoori chicken masala
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp ghee

Nutritional Info (Approx)

Calories: 395 kcal
Protein: 55 g
Fat: 17 g
Carbs: 2 g

Instructions

  1. Mix the chicken with all the spices, ginger-garlic paste, and salt.
  2. Heat a pan on medium flame and add the chicken.
  3. Cook for 2½ minutes on each side.
  4. Cover and cook for 7–8 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked.
  5. Remove the lid, add 1 tbsp ghee, and cook for 1 minute while mixing well.
  6. Serve hot with rice, roti, or salad.
