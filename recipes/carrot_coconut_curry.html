Carrot Coconut Curry

A delicious carrot coconut curry.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 15 mins
Total Time 25 mins

Ingredients

  • 2–3 carrots, peeled and finely chopped or grated
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • For Tempering:
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 tsp chana dal
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • A few curry leaves
  • 1–2 green chilies, slit
  • Optional Spice Mix:
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 2–3 cloves
  • 2–3 dried red chilies
  • ¼–½ cup grated fresh coconut

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter. Then add the cumin seeds, chana dal, urad dal, and dried red chilies. Sauté until the dals turn light golden.
  3. Add the chopped onions and cook until soft and translucent.
  4. Add the curry leaves and green chilies. Sauté for about 2 minutes.
  5. Stir in the chopped or grated carrots, turmeric powder, and salt. Mix well.
  6. Cover and cook on low to medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are soft.
  7. Optional: Dry grind the cumin seeds, cloves, dried red chilies, and grated coconut into a coarse mixture (add a little water only if needed). Mix it into the cooked carrots and cook for another 2–3 minutes for extra flavor.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice, chapati, or as a side dish.
