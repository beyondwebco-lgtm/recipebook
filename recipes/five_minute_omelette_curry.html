5-Minute Omelette Curry

A speedy, flavorful egg curry made by pouring a seasoned egg mix directly onto a fragrant spiced onion-garlic masala base. Recipe by @joee_cooks.

Prep Time 2 mins
Cook Time 3 mins
Total Time 5 mins
Servings 1–2
Calories 400 kcal

Ingredients

  • For the eggs:
  • 3 whole eggs
  • ¼ onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • For the curry masala:
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 6–7 curry leaves
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • ¾ onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp salt

Nutritional Info

Calories: 400 kcal
Protein: 21 g
Carbs: 22 g
Fat: 29 g

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In a bowl, whisk together the 3 whole eggs, ¼ finely chopped onion, green chilli, ¼ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp pepper, and ½ tsp salt. Set aside.
  2. Make the masala: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan on medium flame. Add cumin seeds, curry leaves, chopped garlic, and ¾ chopped onion. Sauté until golden.
  3. Add spices: Add Kashmiri red chilli powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and ½ tsp salt. Mix thoroughly.
  4. Add eggs: Pour the whisked egg mixture directly over the sautéed masala in the pan and spread evenly.
  5. Cook: Lower the heat and cook for 1 minute until the bottom of the omelette sets nicely.
  6. Flip & Serve: Carefully flip the omelette, turn off the heat, and let the residual heat cook the top. Serve hot! 🍳
