Aloo Masala Curry (Creamy Tomato Gravy)

A rich, creamy potato curry made with a spiced tomato-coconut-cashew-poppy-seed gravy. Perfectly balanced, aromatic, and comforting. An excellent side dish for flatbreads or rice.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 30 mins
Total Time 45 mins
Servings 3–4

Ingredients

  • Potatoes:
  • 300 g potatoes
  • Water for boiling
  • Masala Paste:
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp coconut powder
  • 8–10 cashews
  • 1 tsp poppy seeds (gasagasalu)
  • Whole Spices & Aromatics:
  • 3–4 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 small cinnamon stick (dalchini)
  • 3–4 cloves (lavangalu)
  • 2–3 cardamom pods (yalakulu)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 green chilies, slit
  • Powdered Spices:
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1–1½ tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Finisher:
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed)

Serving Suggestions

Serve this hot with chapati, naan, roti, or jeera rice. The creamy cashews and poppy seeds make the gravy exceptionally rich.

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes: Pressure cook 300 g potatoes for 3 whistles until tender. Once cooled, peel them and cut into medium-sized cubes. Set aside.
  2. Grind the masala paste: Blend the 2 tomatoes, coconut powder, cashews, and poppy seeds in a blender until it forms a very smooth, consistent paste.
  3. Temper whole spices: Heat 3–4 tbsp of oil in a pan. Add the cumin seeds, cinnamon stick, cloves, and cardamom pods. Sauté for a few seconds until aromatic.
  4. Sauté aromatics: Add the finely chopped onion and slit green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
  5. Cook the gravy: Add the blended tomato-coconut-cashew-poppy paste. Cook on medium heat, stirring regularly, until the oil separates from the masala.
  6. Add spices: Stir in the turmeric powder, red chili powder, roasted cumin powder, and salt. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  7. Combine and simmer: Add the boiled potato cubes and pour in enough water to reach your preferred gravy consistency. Mix gently, cover, and simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  8. Finish with Kasuri Methi: Crush the kasuri methi between your palms, stir it in, and turn off the heat. Serve hot.
