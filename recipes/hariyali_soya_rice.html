Hariyali Soya Rice

A nutritious, aromatic one-pot meal made with soaked soya chunks, fresh mint-coriander paste, and fragrant basmati rice. Recipe by @joee_cooks.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 25 mins
Total Time 35 mins
Servings 3
Calories 505 kcal

Ingredients

  • Green Paste:
  • Handful fresh coriander
  • Handful fresh mint
  • 1-inch ginger (10 g)
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 green chilli
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tsp fennel seeds
  • 150 ml water
  • Rice & Soya:
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, chopped (90 g)
  • 100 g soya chunks, soaked
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 500 ml water
  • 250 g basmati rice, washed
  • Handful fresh coriander

Nutrition (Per Serving)

Serves: 3
Calories: 505 kcal
Protein: 25 g
Carbs: 79 g
Fat: 10 g

Instructions

  1. Make the green paste: In a blender, combine coriander, mint, ginger, garlic, green chilli, cloves, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, and 150 ml water. Blend until smooth and set aside.
  2. Sauté aromatics: Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pot over medium flame. Add chopped onion and soaked soya chunks. Sauté until the onion turns golden/dark brown (about 10 minutes).
  3. Cook paste: Add the blended green paste into the pot and cook until the raw aroma completely disappears.
  4. Season: Stir in garam masala and salt, mixing well with the soya chunks.
  5. Add rice and water: Pour in 500 ml water and add the washed basmati rice. Mix gently and bring the mixture to a rolling boil.
  6. Simmer: Once boiling, sprinkle a handful of fresh coriander on top. Cover tightly with a lid, reduce heat to low, and cook for 25 minutes.
  7. Fluff and serve: Turn off heat, fluff the rice gently with a fork, and serve warm. 🌿🍚
